Summary:
The page is the Tax Administration’s central portal for tax explanations and interpretations (Podatki.gov.pl). It aggregates guidance for individuals, businesses, and various tax topics, including:

- Personal taxes (od dochodu) and different income situations (employment, foreign income, commissions, IP, rental income, unregistered activity, pensions) 
- Taxes on assets (SD – spadki i darowizny) and wealth changes
- Taxes on purchases (transactions domestically and abroad)
- Other taxes (local taxes and other forms of taxation)
- Key sections for entrepreneurs ( podstawowe podatki dla przedsiębiorców; inne podatki such as PCC, SD, royalties from certain minerals, financial institutions, alcohol taxes)
- Compliance and regulatory tools (WHT, MDR, ICAP, ETACA, SME procedures, GLOBE, CRBR)
- Services to manage tax affairs online and related guidance
