The page "Serwis o podatkach - Interpretacje ogólne" on podatki.gov.pl serves as the central gateway for general tax interpretations and guidance from the Polish Ministry of Finance. It outlines: 

- Main tax areas: personal taxes (PIT), taxes for entrepreneurs (CIT, VAT, JPK), customs, and other guidance resources.
- Sections and tools: guides and information booklets, news, tools, and online services to handle tax matters.
- Tax categories by situation: 
  - Personal income tax (PIT) with basics, allowances, rates, forms, and scenarios (employment, abroad work, contract work, royalties, rental income, undeclared activity, nanny income, pension, etc.).
  - Wealth/asset taxes: inheritance and donations, civil-law transactions.
  - Purchases: excise duty, VAT.
  - Other taxes and local taxes: selected local taxes, gaming tax, real estate tax, and related topics.
