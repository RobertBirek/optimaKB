Summary:
- This PDF brochure from podatki.gov.pl (January 2026) explains the structure of JPK_VAT with deklaracją for JPK_V7M(3) and JPK_V7K(3), effective from February 1, 2026.
- It covers:
  - Main schema structure for JPK_V7M and JPK_V7K
  - Headers and Podmiot1 sections
  - Ewidencja (records) section, including general filling principles and specific inventory for Tax Payable (Podatek należny) and Tax Recoverable (Podatek naliczony)
  - Detailed structures for SprzedazWiersz, ZakupWiersz, corresponding control totals (SprzedazCtrl, ZakupCtrl) and their control sums
  - Deklaracja (declaration) section, including header and detailed declaration positions
- The document is an informational guide intended to help entities correctly complete JPK_VAT with the single declaration under the new JPK_V7M(3) and JPK_V7K(3) schemas.
