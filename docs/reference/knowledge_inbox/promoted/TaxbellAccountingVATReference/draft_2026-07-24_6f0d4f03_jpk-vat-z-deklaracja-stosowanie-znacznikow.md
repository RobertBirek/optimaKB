This page from podatki.gov.pl explains how to use “JPK_VAT z deklaracją” with the ewidencyjna part (data of invoices) when issuing JPK_VAT with declaration. It focuses on marking invoices that involve Krajowy System e-Faktur (KSeF) and the scenarios you must indicate in the field “Dane z faktur lub oznaczenia dotyczące występowania faktur w KSeF” for sales.
Key points:
- When to fill the KSeF indicator: if invoices have a KSeF number by the filing date (even if sent via offline mode, during outage, or system unavailability). In such cases, mark the entry with the KSeF number for all modes.
- Exemption scenario: if the invoice was issued under outage conditions and has no KSeF number, you may use the indicator for invoices without KSeF, per art. 106nf(1).
