Summary:
Poland’s Ministry of Finance invites taxpayers to consultation on the logic structures JPK_V7M(3) and JPK_V7K(3) linked to JPK_VAT with declaration, ahead of their 1 Feb 2026 implementation alongside the mandatory KSeF system. Feedback on the new structures can be submitted by July 8, 2025, at konsultacje.jpkvat@mf.gov.pl. The drafts are available in the Tax Consultations section of the Ministry’s website. Target audiences: taxpayers using monthly or quarterly settlement.
