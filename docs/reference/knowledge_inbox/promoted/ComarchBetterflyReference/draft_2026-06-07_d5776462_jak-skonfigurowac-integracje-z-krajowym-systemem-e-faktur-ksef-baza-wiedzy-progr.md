Summary:
The page explains how to configure Comarch Betterfly to integrate with the National System of e-Invoices (KSeF). Steps include:
- Open Settings and go to the KSeF section.
- Choose the appropriate KSeF environment.
- Select an authentication method: either Ministry of Finance certificate (generated in the KSeF Taxpayer App; token-based access expires after a week) or a Token (generated in the KSeF Taxpayer App; usable until end of 2026). If multiple users access the program, each user must have their own account and install their own certificates.
