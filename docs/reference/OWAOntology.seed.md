# OWA Platform Optima Ontology Seed

Purpose:

- global ontology for Comarch ERP Optima in the OWA project
- bridge business entities, OptimaMCP tools, and MSSQL structures
- capture canonical entities, field mappings, relations, and workflow patterns

Current source of truth:

- draft corpus under `downloads/knowledge_inbox/2026-07-15/`
- resubmitted draft corpus under `downloads/knowledge_inbox/2026-07-17/`

Planned entity slices:

- master data
- trade documents
- warehouse documents
- settlements and accounting
- HR / payroll
- CRM
- analytics and workflow

Important constraint:

- do not ingest live business rows as KB facts
- only ontology summaries, verified mappings, and safe structural abstractions
